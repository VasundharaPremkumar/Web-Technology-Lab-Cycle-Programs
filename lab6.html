JSS SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY UNIVERSITY

About the University

JSS Science and Technology University (JSS STU), Mysuru is one of the leading technical universities in Karnataka. The university provides quality education, research opportunities and industry-oriented learning.

Courses Offered

  • Computer Science and Engineering
  • Information Science and Engineering
  • Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
  • Electronics and Communication Engineering
  • Mechanical Engineering
  • Civil Engineering

Facilities

Digital Library, Smart Classrooms, Modern Laboratories, Research Centers, Sports Complex and Placement Cell.
